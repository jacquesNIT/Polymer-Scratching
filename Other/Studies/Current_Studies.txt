Scratching time comparison:

-> Tests for 0.05s and 0.1s

MR and VE :
    - Both high AE/IE and ALLPW for finer mesh only (0.01 and 0.007)
    - Bad reproductibility of SCOF and Pile-up. Might be affected by Energy conservation
    - Alright reproductibility for RF2

    For MR
    - ALE decreases the overall stability of the simulations while giving results that are approximately the same. Visually, the absence of heavy distortion explains that ALE is not needed
    - SCOF goes below mu_input for 0.05s ST
    - While (no ALE - 0.1s) is still relatively unstable, it seems like the best compromise for now

    For VE
    - Influence of ST is felt for SCOF, +0.02 on average for 0.1s, no real conclusion can be made on RF2


    Simulation Time increases 9x for VE, 2x for MR
    ALE increases time by 3x for VE, 1x for MR

Glassy (DP and PMMA) :
    - AE/IE too high for DP, at the limit for PMMA. ALLPW close to fail as well.
    - Very good results reproductibility for SCOF, RF2, Depth and Pile-up
    - No proof of convergence for SCOF, RF2, Depth and Pile-up (important differences between mesh)

    - no ALE has given visually non-physical results, very high distortion of elements as well as weird recovery phenomena 

    For DP
    - ALE completely changes the physic of the simulation, RF2 decreases by 20%, Res depth increases by 20%, Pile-up decreases by 5%

    For PC,
    - ALe also affects the results, RF2 decreases by 20%, Res depth increases by 10%, Pile-up decreases by 10%
    Simulation Time increases 2x
    ALE increases time by 1.2x 

Crys :
    - AE/IE, KE/IE and ALLPW are all good
    - Very good results reproductibility in all cases
    - Relatively close results, but still no proof of convergence for SCOF, RF2, Depth and Pile-up

    Simulation time increases 3x
    ALE increases time by 2x
